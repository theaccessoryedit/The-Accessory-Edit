Choosing by room size

For the mirrors, measure your ceiling and the wall space first: the 76-inch arched mirror suits higher ceilings, while the 58-inch one is easier in a compact room. For storage, a 72-inch sideboard needs real wall length, so tape out its footprint on the floor before you order.
