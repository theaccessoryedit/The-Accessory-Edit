The Cozy Layers Edit: 6 Pieces for a Softer, Warmer Bedroom

A bedroom feels cozy when it has layers: a calm base, a few soft textures, and warm light. Here's one way to build it.

Cozy is mostly layering. Start with a calm, light base, add soft textures on top, then finish with warm lighting. These six picks follow that order, in a neutral palette so you can swap pieces in and out without anything clashing.

A simple order to follow

Start with the bed, because it's the biggest visual element. Add the rug so the room feels grounded, then use warm bulbs, around 2700K, in the lamps. Dim them in the evening and the room will feel noticeably softer.
