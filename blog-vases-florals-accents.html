Vases, Faux Florals and Shelf Accents: 7 Finds for a Styled Look

The easy formula for a styled shelf or console: a vase or two, a few stems that never wilt, and one small sculptural object.

A shelf or console looks styled when it has three things: height (stems or a tall vase), weight (a solid piece like a planter or a vase with presence), and one small object to catch the eye. Everything below follows that formula and sticks to a soft, neutral palette, so the pieces mix and match.

If you'd rather buy fewer vases and keep them for years, start with our short case for buying fewer, better vases.

How to put it together
